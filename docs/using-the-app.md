# Using the app

## Build a circuit
- Pick a category in the sidebar (Sources, Passive, Switches…) or use **Find a part**, choose a part, then click the
  canvas where it should go. Escape cancels.
- Click a pin, then another pin, to draw a wire between them. While wiring, click an existing wire to join it in the
  middle.
- Click a part to see its readings and change its values in the panel on the right; the dial beside it turns the main
  value up and down.
- Hover a wire for its voltage and current.
- **Show on scope** adds the selected part to the oscilloscope.
- **Example** loads a ready-made circuit; **New** starts an empty one.

## Edit
- Shift-click or Shift-drag selects several parts.
- Ctrl/⌘ + A, C, V, Z: select all, copy, paste, undo.
- Ctrl/⌘ + B narrows the sidebar to icons and back.

## Keyboard
- Tab moves between parts, wires and notes.
- Enter or Space acts like a click: it selects, flips a switch or holds a push button.
- The arrow keys move the selected part one grid step.
- Delete removes it, R rotates it, Escape clears the selection.

## Touch screens
One finger pans, two fingers pinch to zoom, and press and hold a part (or the background, then drag) to select
several. The panel has Copy, Paste and Delete for them.

## Save and share
- **Library** (or Ctrl/⌘ + S) saves circuits in your browser and opens or saves `.circuit.json` files.
- In Chrome, Edge and the desktop app, "Save to <file>" writes the opened file again in place; other browsers
  download a copy.
- **Library → Picture** saves an SVG or PNG image of the circuit.
- The circuit you're editing is kept when you reload the page.
